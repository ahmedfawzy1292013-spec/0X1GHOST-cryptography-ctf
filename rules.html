0X1GHOST CRYPTOGRAPHY — RULES

Please read the rules before solving challenges.

01

Flag Format

Every flag on this platform follows the exact format: 0X1GHOST{...}
Submissions are checked case-insensitively, but they must still match this format.

If your answer doesn't look like this, you haven't found the flag yet.
02

No AI Assistance

Using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or any other AI assistant to solve challenges is strictly forbidden.

Every challenge is meant to be solved with your own knowledge, research, and tools.
  • CyberChef
  • Python scripts
  • Online cipher solvers you understand and control
  • Your own cryptography knowledge
03

Goal of This Platform

The long-term goal of 0X1GHOST Cryptography is to reach 100 cryptography challenges.

Challenges will cover:
  • Encoding
  • Classical Ciphers
  • XOR
  • RSA
  • AES
  • Hashing
  • And beyond

Difficulty levels:
  • Easy
  • Medium
  • Hard
04

Fair Play

Do not share flags with other players.

Do not attempt to read the flag directly from the database or application files instead of solving the challenge.
05

Have Fun

This platform exists to help you learn cryptography by doing.

Take your time, research the concepts, and enjoy the process.
